A. Classify polynomials (6.01)

Terms are separated by + or −. Name by COUNT and DEGREE.

Multi-variable degree = SUM of exponents. 3y3x has degree 3 + 1 = 4, not 3.

Standard form: descending degree order.

NOT a polynomial: variable in denominator, fractional/negative exponent on a variable, variable in a root, or variable in the exponent (2x).

B. Add/subtract polynomials + closure (6.02)

Add

(3x3 + 5x − 2) + (x3 − 4x + 7) = 4x3 + x + 5. Just combine like terms.

Subtract — the trap

(6x2 + 2x3 − 3) − (4x3 − x2 + 5) = 6x2 + 2x3 − 3 − 4x3 + x2 − 5 = −2x3 + 7x2 − 8.

Distribute the minus to EVERY term of the second polynomial. The +5 becomes −5; the −x² becomes +x².

Closure: + and − and × of polynomials always give a polynomial. Division does NOT (can introduce variables in denominators).

(f + g)(a) = f(a) + g(a); (f − g)(a) = f(a) − g(a).

Word problems: "profit = revenue − cost" → answer is a PROFIT, not a cost. "Subtracted FROM" reverses the order: "A subtracted FROM B" means B − A.

D. Multiplying polynomials (6.04)

Distribute a monomial

−3x2(5x − 4x2 − 6) = −15x3 + 12x4 + 18x2 = 12x4 − 15x3 + 18x2 (standard form).

FOIL two binomials

(2x − 3)(x + 4) = 2x2 + 8x − 3x − 12 = 2x2 + 5x − 12.

Two special products (memorize these)

Difference of squares

(a − b)(a + b) = a2 − b2. Middle terms cancel.

(8x − 4)(8x + 4) = 64x2 − 16.

Perfect square — the trap

(a ± b)2 = a2 ± 2ab + b2.

(3x − 8)2 = 9x2 − 48x + 64 — don't drop the −48x middle term! Common wrong answer: 9x2 − 64.

Binomial × trinomial: distribute each term of the binomial across the whole trinomial → 6 products → combine like terms.

E. Function composition (6.05)

f(g(x)) means: do g first, then apply f to the result. Inside function runs first.

Numeric: f[g(7)]

f(x) = 4x + 21, g(x) = 2x + 2. g(7) = 16. f(16) = 4(16) + 21 = 85.

Algebraic: f[g(x)]

f(x) = 6x + 11, g(x) = x + 6. f[g(x)] = f(x + 6) = 6(x + 6) + 11 = 6x + 47.

Order matters: f(g(x)) ≠ g(f(x)) in general.

Word problems — pick the right order

Sale then tax: discount c(p) = 0.7p happens FIRST → c is INSIDE. Tax f(c) = 1.12c on top → f outside. Composition: f[c(p)] = 1.12(0.7p) = 0.784p.

Units of a composite

Chain the units. If g goes weeks→hours and f goes hours→signs, then s(w(h)) goes weeks→signs, i.e., signs per week.

Squared-binomial trap: k(p(x)) where k has x2. k(x − 4) = 2(x − 4)2 − 8 = 2(x2 − 8x + 16) − 8 = 2x2 − 16x + 24. Keep the −16x middle term.

🔑 The Module 6 cheat sheet

  • Multi-var degree: SUM of exponents in each term.
  • Subtract: distribute the minus to every term of the second polynomial. ALL signs flip.
  • Closure: +, −, × of polynomials always give a polynomial. ÷ does not.
  • Exponent rules: same base × → ADD exponents; ÷ → SUBTRACT; (am)n → MULTIPLY. Power of a product applies to the coefficient too.
  • Perfect square: (a ± b)2 = a2 ± 2ab + b2. Don't drop 2ab.
  • Difference of squares: (a − b)(a + b) = a2 − b2. No middle term.
  • Composition: inside function first. Order matters. Word problems: the action that happens FIRST is innermost.
